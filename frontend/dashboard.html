Terms of Service

Last updated: June 2025

1. Acceptance

By using CloudConsultant Auditor, you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

2. Description of Service

CloudConsultant Auditor is a self-hosted security report generation tool that parses Prowler, ScoutSuite, and AWS Security Hub JSON output and produces professional PDF reports. All processing occurs on your own infrastructure — no data is transmitted to CloudConsultant Auditor servers.

3. License Tiers and Report Limits

  • Free: 3 lifetime analyses per device. No card required.
  • Freelancer ($79/mo): 15 reports per calendar month, 1 seat.
  • Consultant Pro ($199/mo): 60 reports per calendar month, up to 3 seats.
  • Pay Per Report ($5.99): 1 report per purchase, no expiry.

Report counts reset on the first day of each calendar month. Unused reports do not roll over.

4. Payments and Refunds

Payments are processed by Polar.sh. You may cancel at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Pay-per-report purchases are non-refundable once the report has been generated. For billing disputes, contact support@cloudconsultantauditor.io within 14 days.

5. Acceptable Use

You may only audit AWS environments for which you have explicit authorisation. You may not:

  • Process data from environments you do not own or have written permission to audit.
  • Reverse-engineer, resell, or redistribute the Service.
  • Use the Service in violation of any applicable law or regulation.

6. Data and Privacy

CloudConsultant Auditor processes your uploaded JSON files in memory only. No finding details, resource names, or ARNs are stored. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Short version: Your uploaded security scan files are processed in memory and never stored. The only data we retain is an anonymised scan summary (score + severity counts) for trend charts. No finding details, resource names, or ARNs are ever saved.

1. What Data We Collect

Uploaded JSON files: Parsed in memory. Never written to disk, never transmitted to third parties, discarded immediately after the response is sent.

Scan summaries: Date, security score, grade, total finding count, and severity breakdown only. No check IDs, resource names, ARNs, or account IDs.

License keys: Stored as SHA-256 hashes only. The raw key is never persisted.

Free-tier fingerprint: A hash of your IP + User-Agent, used only to enforce the free limit. Not linked to any personal identity.

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • No AWS account IDs, ARNs, or resource names from your scan files
  • No finding details, check IDs, or remediation content
  • No cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts
  • No email addresses (unless you contact us directly)
  • No payment card data (handled entirely by Polar.sh)

3. Data Storage Location

All data is stored on the server where you deploy CloudConsultant Auditor. The only external services contacted are Polar.sh (license validation) and Supabase (license/session storage in the hosted version).

4. Third-Party Services

Polar.sh: Handles payment processing and license key issuance.

Supabase: Used for license key and session storage in the hosted deployment.

5. Data Retention

  • Scan summaries: retained for up to 12 scans per company name, then overwritten.
  • License key hashes: retained until the subscription is cancelled.
  • Session tokens: expire after 72 hours.

6. Your Rights

To delete your scan history, remove the scan_history/ folder from your deployment. To delete your license record, contact support@cloudconsultantauditor.io with your license key prefix (first 8 characters).

7. Security

All connections use TLS. License keys are stored as SHA-256 hashes. Session tokens are cryptographically random 32-byte values. The backend never logs uploaded file contents.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: support@cloudconsultantauditor.io
