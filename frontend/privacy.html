Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Short version: Your uploaded security scan files are processed in memory and never stored. The only data we retain is an anonymised scan summary (score + severity counts) for trend charts. No finding details, resource names, or ARNs are ever saved.

1. What Data We Collect

Uploaded JSON files: Parsed in memory during your request. Never written to disk, never transmitted to third parties, discarded immediately after the response is sent.

Scan summaries: After each report generation, we store a small JSON file containing: date, security score, grade, total finding count, and severity breakdown (critical/high/medium/low counts). No check IDs, resource names, ARNs, account IDs, or finding descriptions are stored.

License keys: Stored as SHA-256 hashes only. The raw key is never persisted. Used solely to validate your subscription tier and enforce report limits.

Free-tier fingerprint: A hash of your IP address and browser User-Agent, used only to enforce the 1-report free limit. Not linked to any personal identity.

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • No AWS account IDs, ARNs, or resource names from your scan files
  • No finding details, check IDs, or remediation content
  • No cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts
  • No email addresses (unless you contact us directly)
  • No payment card data (handled entirely by Polar.sh)

3. Data Storage Location

All data is stored on the server where you deploy CloudBrief — either your local machine or your own cloud instance. CloudBrief does not operate a central cloud service that receives your data. The only external service contacted is Polar.sh (for license key validation) and Supabase (for license/session storage if you use the hosted version).

4. Third-Party Services

Polar.sh: Handles payment processing and license key issuance. Subject to Polar's Privacy Policy.

Supabase: Used for license key and session storage in the hosted deployment. Data is stored in the EU/AP region depending on your deployment. Subject to Supabase's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

  • Scan summaries: retained for up to 12 scans per company name, then overwritten.
  • License key hashes: retained until the subscription is cancelled.
  • Free-tier fingerprints: retained indefinitely (small record, no PII).
  • Session tokens: expire after 72 hours.

6. Your Rights

To delete your scan history, remove the scan_history/ folder from your deployment. To delete your license record or free-tier fingerprint, contact support@cloudbrief.io with your license key prefix (first 8 characters).

7. Security

All connections use TLS. License keys are stored as SHA-256 hashes. Session tokens are cryptographically random 32-byte values. The backend never logs uploaded file contents.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: support@cloudbrief.io
